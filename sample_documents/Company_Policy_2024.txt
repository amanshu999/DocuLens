Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Department: Human Resources & Operations

1. REMOTE WORK & EQUIPMENT STIPEND
All full-time employees working remotely are entitled to an annual equipment stipend of $1,500. This stipend covers home office furniture, high-speed internet, ergonomic accessories, and computing peripherals. Reimbursable claims must be submitted within 45 days of expenditure.

2. ANNUAL PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time corporate personnel accrue 15 days of annual paid time off per calendar year. Unused PTO can carry over up to a maximum of 5 calendar days into the following fiscal year. Any additional accrued days beyond this limit expire automatically on December 31.

3. RESIGNATION & NOTICE PERIOD
Standard voluntary departure requires a 30-day written notice submitted directly to the department manager and Human Resources. During this transition window, outgoing personnel are required to complete handover documentation and transition ongoing project deliverables.

4. HEALTHCARE BENEFITS & COVERAGE
The company provides comprehensive medical and dental insurance. The organization covers 100% of the monthly insurance premium for the employee and 50% of the premium for verified dependents.
